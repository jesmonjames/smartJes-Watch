CUSTOM WATCHFACE / CONCEPT 001

smartJO

Designed around a 240 × 296 pixel canvas with a high-tech sci-fi HUD. Features active radar sweep, orbital telemetry blip, holographic scan beam, breathing biometrics, 3D wrist tilt, body-movement wake, full expanded compass navigation screen, and Cyan / Blue-Green / Red Stress themes.

*Sensor capabilities: If your laptop has built-in motion sensors, physical laptop movement triggers the motion wake and increments steps! Real laptop battery level is also read via Battery API. Heart rate (BPM) is simulated as laptops lack optical pulse sensors.

This prototype runs inside your browser to test the 240 × 296 UI layout. It cannot read the physical sensors on a watch strapped to your wrist without an active Bluetooth companion bridge.
